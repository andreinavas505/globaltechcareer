WHAT CHANGES

From describing tasks to communicating security impact.

Your resume and interview stories should help a hiring team understand how you think and contribute in real security environments.

GENERIC DESCRIPTION

“Monitored security alerts, used SIEM tools, investigated incidents and supported security operations.”

EVIDENCE-LED POSITIONING

“Describe the incident context, your investigation and containment decisions, the systems involved, your role in the response, and the verified outcome.”

The example illustrates a writing approach. Use only outcomes and metrics you can substantiate from your own experience.
