Privacy & Data Requests

To exercise an applicable privacy right, email support@globaltechcareer.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” Include your country/state, the right you wish to exercise, and enough information for us to locate relevant records. Do not email passwords, full payment-card numbers, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We may ask for reasonable identity verification and additional details. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain the reason where required and provide any available appeal or complaint route.

For Colombia data-subject requests, use the subject “Solicitud de privacidad / Habeas Data.” The rights and procedures are described in our Política de Privacidad en español.
