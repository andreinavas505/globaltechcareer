THE REAL CHALLENGE

Platform Engineering hiring is about the experience you enable—not just the infrastructure you operate.

Hiring teams want to understand how your platform reduces cognitive load, standardizes safe delivery, improves developer flow and creates measurable value for engineering teams.

◎

Infrastructure is mistaken for a platform

Explain the internal customer journey, self-service capabilities, golden paths, service catalog and platform product model.

↗

Developer impact is hard to quantify

Connect platform capabilities to onboarding time, lead time, deployment friction, adoption, reliability and operational toil.

◇

Architecture answers lack product thinking

Prepare to discuss platform APIs, multi-tenancy, governance, paved roads, extensibility and adoption trade-offs.

THE EVIDENCE-LED METHOD

Position yourself as an engineer of the developer experience.

Build a coherent story that connects platform capabilities to developer workflows, operational guardrails and outcomes for the teams consuming the platform.

  • ✓Target roles by platform ownership, developer audience, cloud scope and operating model.
  • ✓Map platform capabilities: provisioning, service catalog, CI/CD, secrets, observability and policy guardrails.
  • ✓Document adoption, self-service usage, onboarding friction and developer workflow improvements.
  • ✓Prepare architecture stories for Kubernetes, platform APIs, golden paths, multi-tenancy and governance.
  • ✓Show how you balance paved roads with flexibility, reliability, security and cost.

MAKE YOUR EXPERIENCE CLEAR

From infrastructure delivery to platform value

The examples below are illustrative. Replace them with facts and outcomes you can verify from your own work.

GENERIC DESCRIPTION

“Built Kubernetes clusters and maintained Helm charts and internal tools for developers.”

Describes tools and maintenance, but not the internal customer problem or the value created.

EVIDENCE-LED DESCRIPTION

“Designed a self-service deployment path with reusable templates, policy guardrails and documented ownership, reducing manual handoffs and making service onboarding more consistent across engineering teams.”

Connects platform capabilities to developer workflow. Add verified adoption, onboarding or lead-time metrics when available.
